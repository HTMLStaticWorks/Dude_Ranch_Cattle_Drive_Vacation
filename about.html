Our Heritage

Our Story

Since 1892

A Legacy Written in the Dust

WildWest Ranch was founded on the belief that the spirit of the American West should be preserved, not just in books, but through immersive, authentic experience.

The Vision

Our founder, Silas Walker, saw the disappearing frontier and dedicated 10,000 acres to keeping the cowboy lifestyle alive.

The Stewardship

We practice regenerative ranching, ensuring the land remains as wild and pristine for the next hundred years as it was for the last.

The Adventure

Every guest becomes part of our story, contributing to the daily rhythm of life on a working luxury ranch.
